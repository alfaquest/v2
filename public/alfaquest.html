How to play

Each submission must start with the required letter from previous entries. Starting letters cannot repeat. W and X are ignored — no countries begin with those letters. Submissions must be valid country names from the list.

Objective: Complete all 24 required starting letters before you reach a dead-end.

    Scoring system

    How your score works

    Each answer adds points to your run score. Your on-pace rating updates live as you play.

    1. Raw points = (starting letter value × 2) + position bonus (move number × letter tier ÷ 4) for each submission. Rare starting letters earn more position bonus.
    2. Time factor adjusts raw points based on run time vs target (12 seconds per answer, minimum 120 seconds), capped between 0.90 and 1.10.
    3. Speed bonus rewards fast typing per country. The panel shows raw bonus → score contribution (e.g. 45 → +1,800 pts).
    4. Final score = 60% letter/raw influence + 40% timing influence. The number beside each country is how much that answer added.

    Rating bands

    Your on-pace rating shows during the run. The final rating is locked in when all 24 starting letters are completed.

    • Legendary — 35,500+
    • Excellent — 34,000-35,499
    • Very good — 33,500-33,999
    • Good — 33,000-33,499
    • Fair — 31,500-32,999
    • Completed — below 31,500
